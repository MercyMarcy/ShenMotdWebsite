Server Rules

  1. Be Respectful Treat other players with respect. Harassment, bullying, and intentionally provoking others are not tolerated. This is a safe and inclusive server for everyone.
  2. No Slurs or Hate Speech Homophobic or transphobic language, racial slurs, and racism are not allowed. This includes joking about, quoting, or censoring slurs. General swearing is allowed unless used to harass someone.
  3. English Only in Text & Voice Chat Please use English when communicating in text and voice chat so everyone, including staff, can understand conversations and properly moderate the server. Occasional use of other languages is fine, but extended conversations should be kept in English.
  4. No NSFW Discussions Keep all text and voice chats appropriate. Sexually explicit conversations, comments, jokes, roleplay, or other NSFW discussions are not allowed. Please keep these topics out of the server, even if intended as a joke.
  5. No NSFW or Offensive Sprays Sprays containing nudity, revealing clothing, sexual content, or racist content are not allowed.
  6. No Political or Religious Discussions Keep real-world political and religious discussions out of the server.
  7. No Mic Spamming Do not repeatedly play sounds or music in voice chat. To play music or sounds, use proximity chat.

    Options > Audio > Voice > Enable Proximity Chat

  8. No Voice Chat Flooding Avoid overwhelming voice chat with excessive noise, shouting, or loud voices. Do not intentionally talk over others or disrupt communication. Normal conversation overlap is fine, but deliberate disruption is not.
  9. No Cheating or Exploiting or Griefing Cheats, exploits, out-of-bounds areas, and unintended gameplay advantages are not allowed. Don't hold the server captive by taking advantage of bugs and such.
  10. Use Discord for Reports and Appeals Submit player reports, ban appeals, and communication-ban appeals through our Discord.

Server Commands

  • !rtv -- Vote to change the current map.
  • !nominate -- Nominate a map for voting.
  • !calladmin -- Request help from an staff member.
  • /mm or /equip -- Access public and donor models.
